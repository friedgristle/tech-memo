$z=e^{it}\quad(0\leq t<2\pi)$ とおくと, $dz/dt=ie^{it}$ なので, $dz=izdt$.
$$\begin{align*}
\oint z^k dz &=i\oint z^{k+1}dt\\
&=i\oint e^{(k+1)it}dt\\
&=\frac{i}{(k+1)i}\left[ e^{(k+1)it}\right]^{2\pi}_0\\
&=0
\end{align*}$$

$$\begin{align*}
\oint \frac{1}{z}dz &=i\oint dt \\
&=i\left[ t\right]^{2\pi}_0\\
&=2\pi i
\end{align*}$$
